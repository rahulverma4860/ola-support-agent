VIP Customer Handling Policy

Ola's VIP tier includes customers who have completed more than two hundred rides in the past twelve months, corporate account holders with active business travel agreements, and customers manually flagged by the Support Manager as VIP. VIP customers are identified automatically in the ticket system by a badge on their account, and any ticket from a VIP account is automatically upgraded to at least Priority 2 regardless of the original classification. VIP tickets must be handled only by Senior Support Specialists (Tier 2) or above; front-line agents who receive a VIP ticket must immediately reassign it to the correct tier.

VIP customers are entitled to priority queue placement in all support channels, meaning their in-app chat requests are routed to the front of the queue. Service credit and compensation amounts for VIP customers may be approved at up to twice the standard limit by the Senior Specialist without manager sign-off. If a VIP customer's issue is not resolved within the SLA, the Support Manager is notified directly and must personally follow up. VIP accounts are reviewed quarterly and customers who drop below the usage threshold may be demoted from VIP status with a sixty-day notice period.

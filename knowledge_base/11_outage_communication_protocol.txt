Outage Communication Protocol

When Ola experiences a platform outage or service degradation affecting more than five percent of active users, the following communication protocol is activated. Within fifteen minutes of the engineering team confirming the incident, the Support Manager must publish a status update on the Ola in-app status banner and on the official Ola Twitter/X account acknowledging the issue. Updates must be posted at least every thirty minutes until the incident is resolved; the final update must include the root cause in plain language and an estimated prevention plan.

Support agents handling inbound tickets during an active outage must acknowledge the system status in their responses and avoid making promises about resolution times that the engineering team has not confirmed. Ticket SLA timers are paused for all non-VIP tickets during a confirmed outage and resume automatically when the outage is marked as resolved in the internal incident tracker. After resolution, a post-incident summary is shared with the Support and Product teams within forty-eight hours, and affected customers who submitted tickets during the outage receive a proactive service credit applied to their account as a goodwill gesture.

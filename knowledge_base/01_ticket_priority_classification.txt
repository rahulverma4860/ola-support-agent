Ticket Priority Classification Rules

Ola classifies every incoming support ticket into one of four priority levels based on the nature and severity of the issue. Priority 1 (Critical) is reserved for issues that completely prevent a rider or driver from using the platform — such as a rider unable to book any rides due to a payment failure, or a driver locked out of their account — and must receive an initial response within one hour. Priority 2 (High) covers significant service degradation where core functionality is impaired but partially available, such as GPS tracking being inaccurate or a refund not processing, and requires an initial response within four hours. Priority 3 (Medium) applies to non-blocking issues that affect convenience or information access, such as a receipt not being sent or a promo code not applying correctly, and allows a response within twenty-four hours. Priority 4 (Low) is used for general inquiries, feature requests, and feedback submissions, and is addressed within forty-eight hours.

When a ticket is submitted, the support system auto-classifies it based on keywords and category; however, agents are empowered to manually upgrade a ticket's priority if the customer context warrants it. Any ticket that receives three or more escalations from the same account within thirty days is automatically elevated to Priority 2 regardless of the original classification.

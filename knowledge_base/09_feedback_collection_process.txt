Feedback Collection Process

Ola collects customer feedback through three mechanisms: the post-ride rating prompt (one to five stars plus optional comment) displayed automatically in the app within thirty minutes of ride completion, the post-resolution satisfaction survey sent to the customer via in-app notification when a support ticket is closed, and the quarterly Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey distributed to a random sample of active users. Post-ride ratings and comments are used by the Driver Quality team to monitor driver performance, and patterns of negative feedback trigger driver coaching or review. Post-resolution surveys measure support quality and are reviewed by the Support Manager weekly.

Customers may also submit unsolicited feedback at any time through the in-app "Share Feedback" feature; this feedback is tagged as General Inquiry in the ticket system. All feedback data is anonymised before being shared with product teams to protect customer privacy. Customers who rate a support resolution as unsatisfactory (one or two stars) are automatically offered a callback from a senior agent within twenty-four hours to understand and address the ongoing concern.

Service Credit Policy

Ola may issue service credits to customers' Ola wallet accounts as a goodwill gesture when a service failure does not meet the threshold for a cash refund but still caused a measurable inconvenience. Front-line agents are authorised to issue service credits of up to INR 150 (or the local currency equivalent) per incident without manager approval; credits above this amount require Tier 2 approval. Credits are applied as a one-time, non-transferable wallet balance and expire ninety days from the date of issue if unused.

Service credits are appropriate for situations such as the app crashing mid-booking, a confirmed driver cancelling with less than two minutes' notice on a rainy day, or a promotional discount that failed to apply due to a system error. Service credits may not be issued for incidents already resolved with a full cash refund, and a single account may not receive more than three service credits within any thirty-day period — accounts exceeding this limit require manager review before additional credits can be issued. All service credit issuances are logged automatically in the customer's account record for auditing.

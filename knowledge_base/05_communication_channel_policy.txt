Customer Communication Channel Policy

Ola provides support through three primary channels: in-app chat, email (support@ola.com), and a dedicated phone line for Priority 1 and Priority 2 issues. In-app chat is available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, and is the recommended channel for most inquiries because it automatically links the conversation to the customer's ride history. Email support is monitored during business hours and has a target first-response time of four hours; customers submitting Priority 3 or 4 tickets via email may be redirected to in-app chat for faster service.

The phone support line is reserved for critical and high-priority issues; customers calling about Priority 3 or 4 issues will be asked to use in-app chat or email to keep the phone line available for urgent cases. All support interactions are logged in the customer's account record regardless of channel, ensuring continuity if a customer switches channels mid-case. Agents must never share personal contact information with customers and must keep all interactions within official Ola channels to ensure quality monitoring and data security compliance.

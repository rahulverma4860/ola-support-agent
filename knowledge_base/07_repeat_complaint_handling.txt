Repeat Complaint Handling Policy

When the same customer submits three or more tickets on the same root issue within a thirty-day rolling window, the system automatically tags the account as a repeat complainant and notifies the senior support specialist. The senior specialist must review the previous ticket history before responding, ensure the root cause was genuinely addressed, and not simply close the ticket with a standard reply. If the underlying problem was not properly resolved in previous interactions, the repeat ticket is immediately upgraded in priority and a new resolution plan is communicated to the customer within two hours.

Agents are not permitted to close a repeat-complainant ticket without first completing a root-cause note in the ticket system explaining why the issue recurred. If a customer submits five or more tickets on the same issue within sixty days, the case is reviewed by the Support Manager who may escalate internally to the product or engineering team to investigate a systemic problem. Repeat complaints that appear to result from the same driver or vehicle are flagged to the Driver Quality team for separate review. Customers identified as repeat complainants are not penalised or deprioritised — the goal is to identify recurring platform problems and fix them.

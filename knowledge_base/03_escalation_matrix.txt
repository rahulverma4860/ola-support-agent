Escalation Matrix

Ola's escalation matrix defines who is responsible for resolving a ticket at each tier and under what conditions escalation to the next tier must occur. Tier 1 (Front-line Agent) handles all new tickets and resolves those within their documented authority, such as issuing a standard refund up to the approved amount or resetting a password. A ticket must be escalated to Tier 2 when it cannot be resolved within the Priority SLA, when the customer requests to speak to a supervisor, when the issue involves a safety incident, or when the refund or compensation amount required exceeds the Tier 1 agent's approval limit.

Tier 2 (Senior Support Specialist) handles complex billing disputes, safety-related incidents, driver complaints about unfair deactivation, and cases requiring coordination with the engineering or operations team. Escalation to Tier 3 (Support Manager) occurs when Tier 2 cannot resolve the issue within their defined authority, when the case involves potential legal liability, media attention, or VIP customers, or when a systemic issue affecting multiple accounts is identified. Tier 3 has the authority to issue credits above the standard policy limit, initiate full account reviews, and coordinate with legal, PR, or engineering. All escalations must be documented with a reason code in the ticket before the handoff.

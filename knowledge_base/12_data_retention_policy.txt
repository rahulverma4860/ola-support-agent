Data Retention Policy for Support Tickets

Ola retains support ticket data — including the full conversation history, attachments, and agent notes — for a period of three years from the date the ticket was closed. After three years, ticket records are permanently deleted from primary storage systems; anonymised aggregate data (category, resolution time, satisfaction score) may be retained indefinitely for operational analytics without linking back to any individual customer account. Financial transaction records associated with refunds or credits are governed by local tax and accounting regulations and may be retained for up to seven years regardless of the ticket retention window.

Customers may request access to their own support ticket history under applicable data protection laws; such requests are fulfilled within thirty days of receipt via the in-app "My Data" feature or by contacting the Data Privacy team. Customers requesting deletion of their support history are informed that deletion applies only to primary records and that anonymised aggregate data is retained; financial records associated with refunds cannot be deleted within their legal retention window. Support agents have access to ticket history only during active employment at Ola; access is revoked immediately upon separation and all devices are remotely wiped within twenty-four hours.

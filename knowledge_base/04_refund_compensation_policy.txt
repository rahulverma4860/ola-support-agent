Refund and Compensation Policy

Ola issues refunds or compensation credits when a service failure is verified to be the fault of the platform or a driver. Full fare refunds are granted when a ride is cancelled by the driver after acceptance with no alternative offered, when the driver deviated significantly from the agreed route without customer consent, or when a technical error caused a double charge. Partial refunds (up to 50% of the fare) are issued for verified route inefficiencies, long wait times exceeding the expected wait by more than fifteen minutes, or verified driver-quality complaints such as unprofessional conduct.

Compensation credits are offered in lieu of cash refunds for minor service inconveniences — these credits are applied directly to the customer's Ola wallet and cannot be withdrawn as cash. Refund requests must be submitted within seven days of the ride for eligibility; requests submitted after this window are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and approved only with compelling evidence. Cash refunds are processed within five to seven business days to the original payment method; wallet credits are applied instantly. Fraudulent refund claims are flagged in the account system and may result in temporary or permanent account suspension.
